------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  WHAT THE GAME IS ABOUT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EchoHorror is a horror game that you play in a web browser. You are trapped
in a stone maze deep underground. It is pitch black. There is no map.

Monsters live in the maze. They are blind too. They cannot see you - they
find you only by listening. Every noise you make can bring them to you.

There are six levels. Each level is a new, randomly built maze. To finish a
level you collect key pieces, walk to the exit gate in the far corner, and
survive while it opens.

The game is meant to be played with headphones. A lot of the danger is
something you HEAR before you ever see it.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.  THE BIG IDEA: YOU ARE BLIND, AND SOUND IS YOUR ONLY LIGHT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Most of the screen is black. You only see three things:

  * A very small, dim circle of "flashlight" around you. It faintly shows
    the walls and items right next to you. It always works and it makes no
    noise. (It is bigger on Easy and smaller on Hard.)

  * The maze appearing for a moment when you send out an ECHO. An echo is a
    ring of sound that spreads out from you. Wherever the ring reaches, the
    walls, items, monsters and the exit light up for a couple of seconds,
    then fade back into darkness.

  * Light from a candle, if you place one (see the items section).

Here is the catch, and it is the heart of the whole game:

      EVERY ECHO IS ALSO A LOUD NOISE.

When you send an echo, any monster inside the ring's reach hears it and
comes to the spot where you made the sound. So you constantly choose between
"I need to see where I am going" and "I don't want to be heard."

Silence keeps you alive. Sound lets you see. You need both.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.  YOUR GOAL ON EVERY LEVEL
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. You start in the top-left corner of the maze.
  2. Collect the required number of KEY PIECES (the count is shown at the
     top of the screen as "Keys Found"). One of them is always locked inside
     a humming "Echo Stone" - you have to solve its sound puzzle to get it.
     The rest are lying around the maze.
  3. Walk to the EXIT GATE in the far bottom-right corner.
  4. Press the interact key at the gate. It starts to open - but the noise
     wakes EVERY monster in the maze and they all rush to you. You must
     stay standing at the gate for 8 seconds while it opens.
  5. When it opens you go on to the next level. Finish level 6 and you
     escape... or do you? (See the story.)

How many key pieces you need:

      Level 1: 3      Level 2: 4      Level 3: 4
      Level 4: 5      Level 5: 5      Level 6: 5

The maze gets bigger with every level:

      Level 1: 11 x 11 squares      Level 4: 17 x 17
      Level 2: 13 x 13              Level 5: 19 x 19
      Level 3: 15 x 15              Level 6: 21 x 21

You start every level with a fresh inventory: nothing carries over from the
previous level except the knowledge in your head.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  CONTROLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

KEYBOARD (default keys)

  W A S D  or  arrow keys ... walk
  SPACE ..................... send an echo (costs Sound Energy)
  E ......................... interact (touch a stone, use the gate, close a note)
  1 ......................... use a Heart Relic
  2 ......................... use an Eye Totem
  C ......................... place a candle
  P  or  ESC ................ pause / resume

On the Echo Stone puzzle screen: 1, 2, 3 play the LOW, MID, HIGH notes,
R replays the melody, and E or ESC leaves the puzzle.

On the title screen: SPACE or click to start. On the difficulty screen:
1 = Easy, 2 = Medium, 3 = Hard, C = continue a saved game, ESC = go back.
After dying: SPACE or click to try again.

CHANGING THE KEYS
Open Settings (the gear button in the top-right corner). Under "Keys" click
the key next to an action, then press the new key. If the key is already
used for something else, the two actions simply swap. Walking always stays
on WASD / arrow keys, and ESC / Enter can't be reassigned. "Reset keys"
puts everything back to normal. Your choices are remembered.

PHONE OR TABLET
On a touchscreen the controls appear automatically:
  * A round joystick at the bottom-left - drag it to walk.
  * A big ECHO button - same as SPACE.
  * An "E" button - same as the interact key.
  * Three small buttons HRT / EYE / CNDL - Heart Relic, Eye Totem, Candle.
  * The pause and settings buttons at the top-right.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.  THE SCREEN: WHAT EVERY BOX AND BAR MEANS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TOP ROW
  Level ............ which level you are on, out of 6.
  Keys Found ....... key pieces you hold, out of the number you need.
  Threat Level ..... how much danger you are in right now:
        LOW ............... nobody is looking for you.
        SUSPICIOUS ........ at least one monster heard something and is
                            walking over to check.
        HIGH (HUNTED) ..... at least one monster is actively chasing you.

BOTTOM ROW
  Inventory ........ four boxes with a number in the corner:
        KEY (key pieces), CNDL (candles), HRT (Heart Relics), EYE (Eye
        Totems). A box lights up with a red border when it is usable.
  Sound Energy ..... a bar that shows how many echoes you can afford (next
                     section). Green = plenty, yellow = getting low,
                     red = almost empty.
  Controls ......... a reminder of your current keys.

TOP-RIGHT BUTTONS
  Pause, Settings (gear) and Sound On/Muted. Muting is remembered.

MESSAGES
  Short messages appear near the top ("Key piece found (2/3)", "The gate is
  sealed..."). The first time something new happens, a longer tip appears
  once - for example the first time you pick up a candle. You will never see
  the same tip twice unless you press "Show hints again" in Settings.

THE "PRESS E" PROMPT
  When you stand close to the gate, an Echo Stone or a Memory Shrine, a small
  box near the bottom tells you which key to press.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.  THE SOUND PULSE (YOUR ECHO) AND SOUND ENERGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Pressing the echo key sends out a ring of sound. It spreads out very fast,
lights up everything it passes over, and the lit-up picture slowly fades.

  * How far it reaches, and how long the picture lasts, depends on the
    difficulty (see section 13). On Medium it reaches about 3 to 4 maze
    squares in every direction and the picture lasts about 3 seconds.
  * Any monster within the ring's reach HEARS it and comes to investigate
    the spot where you sent it.

SOUND ENERGY
Echoes are not free. You have a bar of 100 Sound Energy and each echo you
send costs some of it. It slowly refills by itself.

              Cost per echo   Refills          Echoes from a full bar
   Easy            20        18 per second           5
   Medium          30        13 per second           3
   Hard            40         9 per second           2

If you press the key without enough energy you hear a dull buzz and the bar
flashes - nothing is sent (and, luckily, nothing is heard either).

FREE, AUTOMATIC ECHOES
Some things send an echo for you, at no energy cost. They are noisy and they
DO alert monsters, so be careful:
  * The first echo when a level begins (a small tutorial echo).
  * Picking up any item (a small one).
  * Stepping on broken glass (a small one).
  * Using a Heart Relic or an Eye Totem.
  * Getting a wrong note on the Echo Stone.
  * The Echo Stone shattering when you solve it (a loud one).
  * Grabbing a lure key (a HUGE one).
  * Starting to open the exit gate (the biggest one of all).

YOUR FOOTSTEPS
Walking makes a very quiet noise. Monsters only notice it if they are almost
right next to you - so never worry about walking; worry about echoing.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.  THE HUNTERS (THE MONSTERS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The hunters are red-eyed blood monsters. They cannot see. They react only to
sound. A hunter is always in one of these moods:

PROWLING (patrol)
  Nobody has bothered it. It wanders slowly between random spots in the
  maze, walking along the real corridors (never through walls), at less
  than half of its normal speed. You can hear its heavy footsteps.

INVESTIGATING
  It heard an ordinary noise - like one of your echoes. It walks to the
  exact spot where the noise happened, stands there for about 3 seconds
  "looking around", then goes back to prowling. If it hears another noise
  in the meantime, it switches to the new spot.
  If you are standing at that spot when it arrives, you are caught.

HUNTING (chase)
  It heard a LOUD noise - the lure key or the gate. It runs straight at you
  and keeps tracking wherever you move, for 8 seconds. Every time you make
  a noise nearby, or if it gets right beside you, that timer refreshes. If
  you manage to get away and stay quiet, it loses your trail and goes to
  check the last place it heard you (investigating).

STAGGERING
  It just touched a candle flame (section 8). It recoils and freezes for
  about 2.5 seconds, then wanders off, having forgotten about you.

DEAD
  It burned twice. It collapses and vanishes a few seconds later.

BEING CAUGHT
  If a hunter touches you, it's over: a jumpscare, a scream, and "YOU DIED".
  The only things that stop this are:
    * a Heart Relic's protection (6 seconds), or
    * the hunter being staggering from a burn.

HOW FAST THEY ARE
  Compared with you, a hunter on Easy walks at about half your speed; on
  Medium about three quarters; on Hard exactly your speed. They get about
  4% faster with each level, and 30% faster during the final gate escape.
  You cannot outrun a Hard hunter for long - you have to be clever.

HOW YOU HEAR THEM
  Their footsteps are heavy and dragging. The closer one is, the louder and
  clearer it gets. If it is on your left, you hear it in your left ear.
  You can hear them from about 6 squares away. A hunter who is chasing sounds
  louder and stomps faster than one who is prowling - the rhythm itself is a
  warning. If a hunter gets very close (about 2 squares), something also
  whispers right into your ear.

HOW MANY
  Easy: 1 hunter. Medium: 2. Hard: 3. Deeper levels add more (section 14).


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.  EVERY ITEM AND OBJECT YOU CAN FIND
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every level scatters these around. You walk over an item to pick it up. Each
pickup makes a small echo (and a little noise), so grabbing things is not
quite free.

KEY PIECE (a small gold key)
  You need a certain number of them (section 3) to open the gate. Some
  "keys" are actually LURES - see below. The last key piece isn't lying
  around at all: it's inside the Echo Stone.

LURE KEY (looks EXACTLY like a real key)
  A trap the maze planted. You can't tell it from a real key by looking.
  When you pick it up: a monster growls, a huge echo goes off, and every
  hunter within about 10 squares starts HUNTING you. There is one on every
  level. Reading the notes (below) hints that "some keys are lures." The
  only defence is to be ready to run, use a candle, or use a Heart Relic.

CANDLE  (you find 2 per level, and the Memory Shrine gives one more)
  Press the candle key to put a lit candle on the floor where you stand.
    * It burns for 18 seconds, then goes out.
    * It lights a warm circle of about 1.5 squares around it: you see the
      walls there, and any hunter standing inside the light.
    * Any hunter that TOUCHES the flame is scorched: it staggers for
      2.5 seconds and forgets about you. Burn the same hunter twice and it
      dies. After a burn, that hunter can't be burned again for about
      4 seconds, so one candle can't kill in a single sweep.
    * Lighting a candle makes a whisper and a bit of noise, and wakes
      hunters within about 2.5 squares. That makes it a great BAIT:
      light one, hurry away, and let the noise draw a hunter into it.
  Candles don't carry over to the next level. Use them or lose them.

HEART RELIC  (you START each level with 1; one more may be lying around)
  Press the Heart key. For 6 seconds you cannot be caught, and there is a
  red ring around you. The price: your heartbeat pounds loudly, roughly twice
  a second, and every beat pulls hunters within about 4 squares toward you.
  It's a panic button, not a stealth tool. You can't use another while one
  is active.

EYE TOTEM  (one per level)
  Press the Eye key. For 10 seconds all your echoes reach 50% farther, and
  you immediately send one big echo. Great for scouting the maze - but the
  bigger the echo, the more monsters hear it.

CURSED NOTE  (one per level - a page of the story)
  Reading it freezes the game while the page is open. There are six pages,
  one for each level, that explain the rules and the lore. Close it with the
  interact key.

ECHO STONE  (one per level - section 9)
  A small standing slab with three glowing dots. It hums so you can find it
  by ear, within about 5 squares. Solving it gives you a key piece.

MEMORY SHRINE  (one per level - section 10)
  A violet diamond that shows you the way to the exit.

BROKEN GLASS  (several hidden per level - section 11)

EXIT GATE  (the far bottom-right corner - section 12)


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.  THE ECHO STONE PUZZLE, STEP BY STEP
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

It is like the old game "Simon Says", played with sound.

  1. Listen for a faint, glassy humming. It gets louder as you get closer,
     and it comes from the side where the stone is. (If you turn on "Visual
     sound cues" in Settings, a blue arrow points toward it.)
  2. Stand right next to the stone and press the interact key.
  3. The stone plays a melody of LOW, MID and HIGH notes and flashes the
     matching buttons. Just listen.
  4. When it says "Your turn", repeat the melody using keys 1 (LOW),
     2 (MID), 3 (HIGH), or by tapping the buttons. The dots on the panel fill
     in as you get notes right.
  5. Get every note right and the stone gives you a KEY PIECE, then shatters
     with a loud crash (a big echo - be ready).

How long is the melody?
     Easy and Medium: 3 notes.  Hard: 4 notes.
     Then add 1 more note on levels 3 and 4, and 2 more on levels 5 and 6.

The catch:
  * While you are at the stone you cannot walk - but the hunters keep
    moving! You can be caught while solving it.
  * Each correct note is a little noise (hunters within about 2 squares may
    notice).
  * A WRONG note makes the stone scream, a whisper sounds in your ear, an
    echo lights up the maze, hunters come to investigate, and the melody
    plays again from the start.
  * You can press R to hear the melody again, or leave with E / ESC and come
    back later. You can't get stuck.

This puzzle is REQUIRED: one of the key pieces you need is only available
here.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. THE MEMORY SHRINE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A violet diamond glowing softly. Press the interact key next to it, once.

  * A violet, moving dotted trail is drawn on the floor from where you are
    standing all the way to the EXIT GATE, following the real corridors. It
    stays for 15 seconds and fades out.
  * You get a free candle.
  * A big, quiet flash lights up the maze. Unlike normal echoes, this one
    does NOT alert any hunter.
  * After that the shrine goes dull grey - it works only once.

Tip: it shows the way from wherever you stand, so the best time to use it is
when you already have most of your key pieces.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. BROKEN GLASS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Shards of glass are hidden on the floor. You cannot see them in the dark
except as a faint glitter when you are very close, or when an echo passes
over them. If you walk over some, it crunches: a small echo, and monsters
nearby come to investigate. A shard only crunches once. A level has 4 pieces
on level 1, growing up to 14 pieces on level 6.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. THE EXIT GATE AND THE FINAL ESCAPE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The gate is in the bottom-right corner - the opposite side of the maze from
where you start. You can't see it in the dark unless an echo reaches it, the
flashlight is close to it, or it is opening.

  * Press the interact key at the gate WITHOUT enough keys: a message tells
    you how many key pieces you still need. Nothing else happens.
  * Press it WITH enough keys: the gate begins to unseal. A huge echo goes
    off, EVERY living hunter in the maze starts hunting you and will never lose
    your trail, and they move 30% faster.
  * You must stay next to the gate for 8 seconds. A ring around the gate
    fills up and a number counts down.
  * If you step away, the ring drains (a bit faster than it filled). Come
    back and it fills again.
  * When the ring is full the gate opens and you move on to the next level.

So the last 8 seconds of every level is a stand-off. Prepare before you press
it: put candles on the floor around the gate, keep a Heart Relic ready, and
know which way the hunters will come from. On small mazes they arrive fast; on
big ones you may get lucky.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. THE THREE DIFFICULTY LEVELS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sizes below are in maze squares (one corridor square is the width of a
corridor).

                                 EASY      MEDIUM      HARD
   Hunters (level 1)               1          2          3
   Hunter speed vs. yours         50%        73%        100%
   How well hunters hear          poor      normal     sharp
   Flashlight size            ~1.8 squares  ~1.4      ~1.0
   Echo reach                 ~4.7 squares  ~3.5      ~2.5
   Echo picture lasts            4.0 s      2.8 s      1.8 s
   Energy cost of an echo         20         30         40
   Energy refill per second       18         13          9
   Echo Stone melody (level 1)   3 notes    3 notes    4 notes

"How well hunters hear": a hunter notices an echo if it is within about
3 to 3.5 squares of you on every difficulty. On Easy your echo is big but
poorly heard; on Hard it is small but sharply heard.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. HOW THE SIX LEVELS GET HARDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * The maze grows from 11 x 11 to 21 x 21.
  * You need more key pieces (3, then 4, then 5).
  * The Echo Stone melody gets longer (+1 note on levels 3-4, +2 on 5-6).
  * More hunters join: +1 on levels 3 and 4, +2 on levels 5 and 6.
    (Level 6 on Hard has 5 hunters.)
  * Every hunter is about 4% faster per level (+20% by level 6).
  * The creepy whispers in the walls become more frequent as you go deeper,
    and twice as frequent while you are being hunted.
  * More broken glass.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. DYING, RETRYING, PAUSING AND SAVING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DYING
  When a hunter catches you there is a loud jumpscare, then "YOU DIED". Press
  SPACE or click to try again. Retrying builds a BRAND-NEW random maze for the
  same level and you start with a fresh inventory. Your death is counted
  ("Times caught"), and shown on the screen.

PAUSING
  Press P or ESC, or the pause button. Everything freezes and all sound stops.
  Press the same key or click to resume. The game also pauses by itself if you
  switch to another window or tab, so nobody gets caught while looking away.
  (You cannot pause while reading a note or solving a puzzle - just leave those
  first.)

SAVING
  The game saves by itself every time you clear a level. Next time you open the
  game the title screen shows "PRESS C TO CONTINUE - LEVEL X". Continuing skips
  the story and starts you at the beginning of the level you had reached, on the
  same difficulty. If you die and then close the game, you'll continue from
  the start of that level.
  The game also remembers:
    * your settings, key choices, and mute setting,
    * the deepest level you ever reached (shown on the title screen),
    * your best finishing time on each difficulty,
    * which tutorial tips you have already seen.
  All of this is kept in your own web browser. It stays on your computer
  or phone; nothing is sent anywhere. If you clear your browser's stored data,
  it is forgotten.

16. SETTINGS AND ACCESSIBILITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Press the gear button (top-right). Opening it pauses the game.

  VOLUME SLIDERS
    Music ............ the slow, sad background tune during the story
    Whispers ......... the creepy voices in the walls
    Footsteps ........ yours and the hunters'
    Echo Stone sounds  the puzzle notes and humming
    (Turn one to zero to silence just that sound.)

  VISUAL SOUND CUES  (for players who can't hear well, or play muted)
    When switched on:
      * Small red arrows circle you and point toward hunters within about 6
        squares. Brighter = closer.
      * Small blue arrows point toward an unsolved Echo Stone.
      * Text captions appear for important sounds, like "[Growl]",
        "[Whispers]", "[Heavy footsteps]" and "[Humming]".

  KEYS  - change any action's key (section 4).

  SHOW HINTS AGAIN - replays the one-time tutorial tips.

The gear can also be used from the title screen.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
17. THE STORY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are a university student researching urban legends for your thesis. You
find an old tape recording about "The Echo Ritual Site" - an underground
labyrinth where sound was once used to talk to unseen horrors. Curious, you
travel deep into the woods to the coordinates on the tape.

The moment you step inside, a heavy stone slab slams shut behind you. Total
darkness. Total silence. You make a small sound - and for a heartbeat the
walls glow, showing you the maze. You realise you have to collect the key
pieces to unseal the exit... and that you are not alone.

Along the way you find six torn pages, one on each level:
  Page 1: the walls absorb sound and reflect light; silence blinds you, noise
          brings them.
  Page 2: earlier victims tried to mark the walls; the maze erases every trace.
  Page 3: the Heart Relic holds them at bay, but its pulse is loud.
  Page 4: don't trust every key - some are lures.
  Page 5: candles draw whispers and burn the beasts; one key piece sleeps
          inside a singing stone.
  Page 6: the exit needs a violent shock to unseal, takes time to open, and
          every monster will rush you. Do not leave the gate.

THE ENDING
When you finally get through the last gate, you step out into daylight. But
then, from the darkness behind you, a voice calls your name. It is your own
voice, begging for help. "The maze never lets anyone leave."

After the ending there is a final screen with your statistics: difficulty,
time in the maze (with your best time), how many times you were caught, and how
many echoes you sent - plus credits.

18. SURVIVAL TIPS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * Listen first. Before you echo, stop and listen for footsteps. If you hear
    one close by, wait.
  * Send fewer, better echoes. One good echo at a junction beats three panicky
    ones. Remember each echo costs energy AND makes noise.
  * Let the flashlight do the small work: it is silent. Use it to feel your
    way along walls and spot items in a room.
  * Find the Echo Stone early by following its hum. It also tells you roughly
    where the maze's key areas are.
  * Assume any key you find could be a lure. Keep an escape route in mind (and
    a candle ready) before you pick up a key.
  * Candles are both a weapon and a bait. Place one in a corridor, walk away
    around a corner, then make a small noise so a hunter walks straight into it.
  * Save your Heart Relic for emergencies and remember it is loud.
  * Use the Memory Shrine when you already hold most of your keys.
  * Before you open the gate, place candles around it, and have a Heart Relic
    ready. You must hold it for 8 seconds.
  * You can always step back and re-plan: a hunter who loses your trail goes
    back to prowling.
  * You can pause any time (P) to think.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
19. WHAT WAS FIXED AND ADDED IN THIS UPDATE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problems that were fixed
  * The game ran about 2.4 times too fast on 144 Hz screens (and slower on
    slow ones). Player and hunter speed now depend on real time, so the game
    plays the same everywhere.
  * Using a Heart Relic made every hunter in range growl dozens of times a
    second. Growls are now limited, and the heartbeat happens about twice a
    second.
  * Echoes, dust and candle light from a previous level or attempt could still
    show up in the next maze. Everything is now cleared at the start of a
    level.
  * You could slip through the tips (corners) of walls. Walls are now solid
    all around you and around the hunters.
  * Prowling hunters walked straight into walls and got stuck. They now follow
    the corridors.
  * Several leftover, unused pieces of the game were removed (a menu, some
    asset lists, and an unused variable).
  * The game no longer depends on the internet for its tile pictures: they
    are now included in the folder. (An internet copy is kept only as a
    backup.)

Things that used to be missing and now exist
  * Hunters can be fought: candles hurt them, and the "hurt" and "dying"
    pictures that had always been in the art files are finally used.
  * Real chasing: loud noises make hunters hunt you and track you for 8
    seconds. Before this, a "chase" almost never happened.
  * The final escape is now a real stand-off: you must hold the gate for 8
    seconds. Before, you were already standing at the gate, so nothing
    happened.
  * Candles now do something. So does the Memory Shrine (it used to give
    nothing).
  * The Echo Stone is required (one key piece is locked inside), instead of
    being optional.
  * Feedback at the gate: it tells you when you still need key pieces.
  * Levels scale better: more hunters and faster hunters deeper down.
  * Saving and Continue, best level, best times, statistics, and a proper
    ending / credits screen.
  * Pause (with automatic pause when you leave the window).
  * A Settings panel: volume sliders, remembered mute, and key rebinding.
  * Accessibility: visual sound cues (arrows and captions).
  * Phone buttons for Heart Relic, Eye Totem and Candle.
  * Short one-time tutorial tips.
  * One name everywhere: "EchoHorror".
  * A README, a small local web server, automated tests, and a
    code-quality check for the developer.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
20. HONEST LIMITS: WHAT HAS AND HASN'T BEEN CHECKED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

What was checked
  * 23 automated tests run the real game code without a screen: they check
    that mazes are always solvable, hunters behave, candles burn, the gate
    counts down, saving works, pausing works, key rebinding works, and that
    the game runs at the same speed at 60 and 144 frames per second. They were
    run over 30 times in a row with no failures.
  * The game was loaded in a real (headless) Edge browser and started
    correctly: it reached level 1 with the hunters and the pictures loaded.
  * The code passes the code-quality checker with no warnings.

What was NOT checked
  * Nobody has played it by hand start to finish yet. How scary, fair or fun
    the new numbers feel (8 seconds at the gate, 8 seconds of chasing, candle
    burn time, the number of hunters) is a matter of taste and may need
    tweaking after real playtests. All these numbers are easy to change.
  * The phone controls were not tried on a real phone - only the code path was
    tested.
  * The screen layout of the Settings panel on very small phones was not
    inspected visually.
  * The artists of the player and monster pictures are not recorded anywhere.
    Before you share or sell the game, find out who made them and check that
    their licence allows it (see README.md).
  * Your keyboard-key choices only apply to actions; walking is always
    WASD / arrows.
