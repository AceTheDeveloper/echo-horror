EchoHorror

The Echo Maze

You are trapped in near-total blackout. There are no maps. Your dim flashlight only reveals what's right around you — the full maze layout is only ever revealed when sound echoes off the stone walls.

Beware: Sound Pulses cost energy — watch your bar. Blind beasts roam the dark, hunting purely by noise. Candles burn them; the gate hates to be opened.

Tip: Listen for humming Echo Stones. Repeat their melody to earn a key piece — but every note you play is noise.

Best played with sound on — this game relies on audio. Prefer to see it instead? Turn on "Visual sound cues" in Settings (the gear, top-right).
